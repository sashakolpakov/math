\begin{abstract}
We give a conditional proof that every nondegenerate spherical tetrahedron
is scissors congruent to each elementary Regge mate.  The hypotheses are an
explicit theorem package separating published field-level results from the
relative motivic and comparison bridge still required over the rational
parameter space.  Internally we derive the monomial Regge action on angle
phases, rationalize the orientation double cover, prove the finite
four-channel tensor cancellation, and carry out the primitive-kernel and
scissors-group diagram chases.  We explain the relative quadric motive that
the external package is intended to supply, including its three weight
pieces, coproduct, Artin descent, and fibre comparison, without treating
those assertions as consequences of ordinary homology.  An accompanying
Lean development checks the internal algebra and conditional implication
without project axioms; it represents every motivic and scissors-theoretic
input by a named field of the proof contract.
\end{abstract}
